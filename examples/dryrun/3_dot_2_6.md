# Dry run 3: dot 2 to 6, the units linked into one accelerator

A snapshot of 5 Nov 2026 (snax-forge `main` at `a4aab4b`; snax-gvsoc `61d1e8ac`; snax-forge-experiments `e8c4e91`, whose SYN2 tables were synthesized and simulated at snax-forge `f9764d5`). FILL (Claude Code): the commits the runs below ran on. The numbers are what these commits printed; no test checks them (D98).

dot 1 (`2_dot_1.md`) is two accelerators that pass the N products through L1. dot 2 to 6 keep the products inside one accelerator: one group of two units joined by a link, so one streamer set, one START and one control unit (D148, D157). Each is a few editor steps from dot 1's step 8. This page builds the five, runs them, and sets the model's estimate against SYN2's synthesis and simulation of the same designs, the check DRY1 left for after the dry runs.

## 1. The five variants

| Variant | From dot 1's step 8 | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| dot 2 | `link tmp0`, D = 1 | tmp0 goes through a one-beat buffer instead of L1 |
| dot 3 | `link tmp0`, D = 0 | a plain wire |
| dot 4 | `split_reduce` on `sum_map`, `fuse_map`, bind `mac` (`chisel_mac`) on the W lanes, an adder tree on the W partials, `link out_partial`, D = 0 | each lane accumulates its own partial sum; the tree adds the W partials once at the end |
| dot 5 | dot 4 with the final sum `chisel_serial` | the W partials added one a cycle |
| dot 6 | dot 5 with `link out_partial`, D = 1 | a buffer before the serial sum |
